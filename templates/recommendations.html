💡 General Organic Farming Tips

♻️
Compost Everything

Convert all crop residues, kitchen waste, and weeds into compost. Target 5–10 tonnes/acre/year.

🔄
Crop Rotation

Rotate cereals with legumes every season. Legumes fix nitrogen and break pest/disease cycles.

🌾
Mulching

Cover soil with 5–10 cm of straw, dry leaves, or grass clippings to retain moisture and suppress weeds.

🐛
Integrated Pest Management

Use sticky traps, pheromone traps, and natural predators (lady beetles, spiders) before resorting to any spray.

💧
Water Conservation

Use drip/sprinkler irrigation to reduce water use by 30–50%. Rainwater harvesting fills farm ponds for dry spells.

🧪
Soil Testing

Test soil pH and nutrients every 2 years. Contact your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) for free soil testing.
